Build Plan

A stage says when a piece goes down, a builder says who puts it there. New pieces take whichever of each is active; to re-tag pieces already on the board, right-click them.

Stagewhen it goes down
Builderwho puts it there
A build starts with no stages and no builders — + adds one. Double-click a name to rename it · the colour dot recolours the tag · × removes it and leaves its pieces unassigned. Choosing which stage and builder are active, and hiding a tag from the board, are done on the board readout. Export image writes one PNG per stage / builder that has anything built under it.
